How To Play

Your goal is to get rid of all your cards before everyone else.

On your turn, play a card that matches the top discard by color, number, or symbol.

You can also play wild cards at any time and then choose the color.

If you cannot play, click the draw pile to draw cards. After drawing, if you have a playable card, you may still play before your turn ends.

Action cards:

  • Skip: the next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse: the turn direction changes.
  • +2 and +4: the next player draws that many cards unless stacking rules let them answer with the same draw type.

You can make a combo play with cards of the same number or symbol. Shift-select lets you group matching cards for one combo play. Release Shift to play the combo.

P.S. Based on the rules that the developer's friends in middle school taught her, not the official UNO rules.
